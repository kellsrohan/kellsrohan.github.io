About

Independent developer in Australia building self-hosted, zero-dependency web and infrastructure software.

Rohan Martin Kells is an independent developer in Australia. He designs, builds and operates self-hosted, zero-dependency web and infrastructure software, and is the founder and CEO of N8.

The work covers web platforms, cloud infrastructure, networking, telecommunications, automation and interface design. This site is his public professional profile and resume.

For professional enquiries, email support@rohankells.com.
