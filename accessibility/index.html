Accessibility statement

How accessible Rohan Kells is, what has been checked, and how to tell us about problems.

Our aim

Rohan Kells aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. This statement lists only what has actually been checked on this site.

Verified on this site

  • No automated WCAG 2.1 A/AA violations found by axe-core on the pages tested (light and dark themes)
  • A "Skip to content" link is the first item reached with the Tab key
  • Keyboard focus is clearly visible on links and buttons
  • The header menu opens with Enter and closes with Escape
  • The chat panel opens and closes from the keyboard (Escape closes it)
  • The light/dark switch works from the keyboard and reports its state
  • Text and interface colours meet WCAG AA contrast in light and dark themes (checked at build time)
  • Animations and page transitions are switched off when your device asks for reduced motion
  • Page language is set to Australian English
  • Images have text alternatives; decorative icons are hidden from screen readers

Known limitations

  • Checks so far are automated (axe-core) plus scripted keyboard tests on a set of pages; no full manual screen-reader review has been done yet.
  • The downloadable brochure PDF is generated from the web page and may not be fully tagged for screen readers. The brochure web page has the same content.
  • Photo descriptions come from the photo library's titles and may be brief.
  • Links to other websites (such as help services) are outside our control.

Feedback

If something on this site is hard to use, please tell us through the contact form or at contact@rohankells.com. We aim to reply within two business days.
