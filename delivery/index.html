Delivery

Digital products are delivered by email, not by post.

Rohan Kells sells digital products and software. There is no physical shipping and no parcel delivery.

After PayPal confirms the payment, delivery details are sent to the email address used at checkout. That may include a download link, a licence, or instructions to access the product.

If you do not receive those details, check your spam folder, then email support@rohankells.com with the email used at checkout and your PayPal transaction details.

See also the refund policy.
