Encryption & PGP Working

Practical encryption for mail, files and hosts, including OpenPGP where a team already uses it, alongside HTTPS already covered under TLS.

What I do with Encryption & PGP

I help teams use encryption in the places it actually protects them: OpenPGP for mail and files where that is already part of the workflow, and disk or volume encryption on hosts I operate. I do not publish a personal OpenPGP fingerprint on this site. Certificate and HTTPS work is covered on the TLS and ACME card.
